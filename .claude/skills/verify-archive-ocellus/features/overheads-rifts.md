# Overheads, heals and rifts

Ocellus wears a curse overhead, drawn under his HP bar with the cache's head icons: Deflect Melee from P4 tick 8 to 32, Soul Split from 33 to 37, none at 38, Deflect Range from 39 to 60, Soul Split again from 61 to 66 and none at 67, then the same each 60-tick cycle. A player hit of the deflected style lands as 0. While the boss hovers over the inner void, from P4 tick 8 to 29, a melee player cannot reach him and does not swing. Ranged still lands. Two ticks after a transition attack hits an unprotected player, Soul Split heals the boss by the damage it dealt, and a heal splat shows on the boss. From cycle 2, on the tick before each inner void (P4 tick 67, then every 60 ticks), two void rifts rise north of the 5x5, on (2, 2) and (11, 2). The player can kill a rift. A rift dead by the end of tick 77 despawns, and the chat shows "Destroying the void rift empowers you!". Each rift still standing at 78 detonates, and the player takes 14 under the void flare. The damage numbers are stand-ins.

## Sub-features

- `overhead-icon` draws sprite 440 frame 15 (Deflect Melee), 16 (Deflect Range) or 11 (Soul Split) under the boss's HP bar while the phase has that overhead. The lead-in has none, and neither has the tick before each void, S+30 and S+59.
- `deflect` logs `player-hit-judged` for each player hit on the boss with outcome `blocked`, `passed` or `invulnerable`. A hit is judged by the overhead up on the tick it was launched, so a ranged hit launched before Deflect Range rises lands in full. A blocked hit lands as 0. The `deflect` choice selects `block-at-launch` (default) or `none`.
- `hover-melee-reach` keeps a melee attack from reaching the boss while he hovers. The inner-void phase carries boss state `present-out-of-melee-reach`, and a player with a melee weapon who targets him there stands in reach without swinging until he lands at 30. The `hoverMeleeReach` choice selects `unreachable` (default) or `reachable`, which gives the hover the landed state `present-damageable`.
- `soul-split-heal` logs `boss-healed` with cause `soul-split` and the attack's ordinal 2 ticks after an unprotected transition hit, by the damage that hit applied. A protected hit heals nothing. The heal splat stays for 2 ticks.
- `rift-spawn` logs `rift-spawned` for two NPC 16678 rifts, at (-2, -4) and (7, -4) from the boss anchor (4, 6), which is (2, 2) and (11, 2) on the measured platform. They spawn at 67 by default or at 127 with `riftFirstCycle=cycle-3`, and play spawn sequence 14896 with its sound.
- `rift-destroyed` logs each rift whose HP was 0 at the end of tick 77, at the next boss step. The rift plays despawn sequence 14897, and the kill line goes to the chat.
- `rift-burst` logs `rift-burst` at 78 for each standing rift with the hit receipt. The rift plays detonate sequence 14898, and spotanim 4306 plays on the player.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:8002/` and watch the icon under the boss's HP bar change at P4 tick 33, 38, 39, 61 and 67. Attack the boss to see blocked hits as 0. With the melee setup, click the hovering boss and watch the player wait beside him until he lands.
- Pick `Rifts` in the `Focus` selector. The drill reloads, and the rifts appear at 67. Click one to attack it. The chat sits above the focus selector.
- Pick `Transition attacks` or `Boss heals`. Let a transition attack hit without the matching prayer to see the heal.

## Driving it with agent-browser

Preconditions:

- The baseline preconditions in the index hold, and the loadout recipe has run.
- The player stays on spawn (5, 1) until a step below clicks, so the camera matches the pixel offsets.
- A click must reach the page between two game ticks. Each click batches a `wait --fn` on the P4 tick with the `mouse move/down/up`.

- **Overhead icons.** Run `agent-browser open "http://127.0.0.1:8002/?seed=1&focus=rifts"` and wait for P4 tick 0, so the loading cover is gone. Save sound and area sound on and click Restart, as the transition-attack recipe does, then wait for P4 tick 0 and install the audio probe. Give no other input. Screenshot `overhead-5.png`, `overhead-31.png`, `overhead-35.png` and `overhead-45.png` at those P4 ticks. The script counts three icon colours in `ICON_BOX`, from 40 left to 40 right of the canvas centre and from 290 to 190 above it. The colours are the blue deflect arrows, the red of the Deflect Melee blade and the Soul Split arrows, and the orange Deflect Range fletching. Blue with red reads as Deflect Melee, blue with orange as Deflect Range, red alone as Soul Split, and neither as no icon. Each reading must equal the overhead that `log()` has at that tick and the next. The thresholds are 0.2% of the box for blue and red and 0.06% for orange. On the platform the measured blue, red and orange shares were 0, 0 and 0 at 5, 0.538, 0.513 and 0 at 31, 0, 0.636 and 0.012 at 35, and 0.538, 0 and 0.147 at 45.
- **No overhead before each void.** On the same page, install the overhead draw probe with the audio probe. It wraps `drawImage` on 2D canvas contexts and, on each animation frame, records the P4 tick from `snapshot()`, so each tick lists its frames and the `sprites/` images drawn into them. The boss's overhead is the only sprite the page draws. Save `window.__overheadDraws` after 79. Ticks 37, 38 and 39 draw `440-11`, nothing and `440-16`, and 66, 67 and 68 draw `440-11`, nothing and `440-15`. Each tick has at least one frame, and `log()` has overhead `null` at 38 and 67. One run drew 36 frames on every tick from 30 to 69 but 34 on 67. A screenshot taken while the probe read P4 tick 98 showed the HP bar with no icon, between Soul Split at 97 and Deflect Range at 99.
- **Kill a rift.** On the same page, screenshot `rift-spawned.png` at 68. In one batch, wait for 68 and click `NORTH_EAST_RIFT_CLICK_LEFT_PX` (325) left of and `NORTH_EAST_RIFT_CLICK_UP_PX` (52) above the canvas centre, on the north-east rift at (11, 2). The player keeps the default ranged setup and shoots it from spawn. Wait for a `rift-destroyed` event, then wait 600 ms for the chat to poll. Screenshot `rift-destroyed.png` and run `agent-browser --json get text "#game_chat"`, which returns the kill line. Screenshot `rift-burst.png` at 78. `log()` shows `rift-spawned` at 67 on (2, 2) and (11, 2), `rift-destroyed` for `rift-67-1` at 73, and `rift-burst` at 78 from `rift-67-0` with receipt `{requested 14, applied 14, beforeHp 99, afterHp 85, prayer null}`.
- **Hear the rifts.** Save the probe's starts after 79. Each rift sound is a cache frame sound of the rift's sequence, matched by its synthesized length at 22050 Hz from P4 tick 66 to 80 and timed as tick plus phase. Spawn sequence 14896 plays 12643 (7717 samples) 120 ms in, once for each rift at 67.2. Despawn sequence 14897 plays 12640 (11025 samples) 160 ms in, on the `rift-destroyed` tick, at 73.27. Detonate sequence 14898 plays 12641 (14332 samples) at 80 ms and 12642 (26460 samples) at 560 ms, once, for the standing rift, at 78.13 and 78.93. The killed rift plays no detonation. Every start must be on a `running` context, within 0.2 tick of its frame. One run measured 67.25 twice, 73.28, 78.13 and 78.94.

In one batch, wait for 12 and click `RISEN_BOSS_CLICK_RIGHT_PX` (-10) right of and `RISEN_BOSS_CLICK_DOWN_PX` (-160) below the canvas centre, on the risen boss, as the loadout recipe does. ## Gotchas

- In the inner void the risen boss's icon is above the top of the screen at the default camera. The icon shots wait for the landing at 30.
- osrs-sdk has no "I can't reach that!". The trainer's player declines a melee swing at the hovering boss the way the SDK declines any attack, by `attack()` returning false, so the player keeps the boss as its target, spends no attack delay and swings on the tick he lands. The tile it waits on is inner void, and the rifts drill runs no floor damage.
- osrs-sdk draws player overheads where the boss's HP banner covers them. The boss's icon sits under his HP bar, as the footage shows.
- A rift joins the world on the tick after the boss spawns it, because osrs-sdk adds new mobs at the next tick. The click waits for 68.
- The osrs-sdk scythe sends all three hits to a 1x1 target. The trainer's scythe hits a target once for each tile of its width, up to three, so a swing hits a rift once and the boss three times.
- The deflect page wears the melee setup, so only Jest proves Deflect Range blocking.
- The footage shows a white wisp travelling to the boss before the Soul Split heal. No cache spotanim matches it, so the trainer does not draw it.
- osrs-sdk has no chatbox. The kill line goes to `#game_chat`, a page element over the canvas.
- The full-fight page also logs rifts at 67. Its checks stop at 70 and do not read them.
- osrs-sdk plays a frame sound with a cache range only to a listener closer than that range, counting Manhattan tiles less one. The rift sounds have range 7. From spawn, (2, 2) is 4 tiles away and (11, 2) is 7, so the player hears both. A rift 8 or more tiles away is silent.
- 12641 has the same length as the explosion series sound 12638. The rifts drill runs no explosions, and the check reads only P4 ticks 66 to 80.
